## 🏗 Project Status: Historical Prototype

This repository contains **one of the first prototype versions** of AudCat - a minimum viable product (MVP) that I developed and iterated on during my student years. Despite the emergence of modern AI assistants and sophisticated accessibility tools, this project represents an important milestone in my journey and serves as a foundation for future enhancements.

## 🌟 Historical Significance

### 🎓 Student Innovation (2014-2016)
This prototype was born during my university years, at a time when:
- **AI assistants were primitive** - Siri could barely play music, Google Assistant was in its infancy
- **Web accessibility was niche** - Few developers prioritized inclusive design
- **Voice technology was limited** - Most solutions required expensive proprietary software

### 💡 Why This Project Matters
While today's landscape includes advanced AI assistants and comprehensive accessibility frameworks, this prototype demonstrates:
- **Early vision** for web accessibility before it became mainstream
- **Creative problem-solving** with limited technological resources
- **Foundation principles** that remain relevant in modern accessibility design
